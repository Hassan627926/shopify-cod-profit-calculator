About EcomTools

EcomTools is a free online resource created to make e-commerce calculations easier and faster for online sellers.

What We Provide

Our tools focus on practical calculations such as Shopify profit, advertising costs, delivery costs, COD performance, margins and other e-commerce metrics.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide simple tools that help sellers understand their numbers without complicated spreadsheets or paid software.

Free to Use

Our calculators are designed to be accessible without requiring an account or subscription.

Accuracy

Calculator results depend on the information entered by the user. Always verify important business and financial decisions using your own records.
