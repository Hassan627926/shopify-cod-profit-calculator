How Product Pricing Works

Your selling price needs to cover all per-order costs and leave enough money as profit to reach your target margin.

Selling Price = Fixed Costs ÷ (1 − Payment Fee % − Target Profit Margin %)

Example

If your product, shipping, advertising and other costs total PKR 1,550, the payment fee is 2%, and your target profit margin is 30%:

1,550 ÷ (1 − 0.02 − 0.30) = PKR 2,279.41

You would need to charge approximately PKR 2,279 to target a 30% margin under these assumptions.

Why Pricing Matters

Pricing too low can leave little room for advertising, discounts, returns or unexpected costs. A pricing calculator helps you estimate a sustainable selling price before launching or scaling a product.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this calculator include advertising cost?

Yes. Enter your estimated advertising cost per order so the recommended price reflects customer acquisition costs.

What is a target profit margin?

It is the percentage of the final selling price you want to keep as profit after the costs included in the calculation.

Can I use this for Shopify products?

Yes. It can be used to estimate pricing for Shopify and other e-commerce products.
