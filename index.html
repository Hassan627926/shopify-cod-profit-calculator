How to Calculate Shopify Profit

For COD e-commerce, sales alone do not show the complete picture. Profit depends on delivered orders and the costs required to acquire and fulfill them.

Total Sales

Selling Price × Delivered Orders

Product Cost

Product Cost per Order × Delivered Orders

Advertising Cost

Ad Cost per Received Order × Received Orders

Delivery Cost

Delivery Cost per Dispatch × Dispatch Orders

Net Profit

Total Sales − Product Cost − Advertising Cost − Delivery Cost

Why return rate matters

A high COD return rate can increase fulfillment costs and reduce the number of orders that generate revenue. Monitor delivery and return performance together with profit.
